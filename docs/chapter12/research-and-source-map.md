## Concept brief and coverage decisions

**Core claim:** a selected reference breaks with the existing sequence under a stated test. Event qualification and subsequent outcome are different facts.

**Convention:** use confirmed pivots and a completed close strictly beyond a fixed level. A touch is insufficient. Closing outside does not require the entire body outside. This is LearnSMC’s reproducible study rule, not a universal ICT attribution.

**Sequence:** existing map → confirmed reference → written test → completed timestamp → later follow-through/failure → separately specified execution.

**Boundaries:** internal/external scale; no established trend in a range; unknown hidden order ownership; subjective swing selection; no guaranteed fill or retest. Displacement/FVG/retest are separate named-model filters. A confirmed break can subsequently fail without becoming a nonexistent event.

**Worked prices:** illustrative index/five-minute/America_New_York; no historical trading date. Fixed120.00 high. Wick-only close119.00, qualifying close122.00; later hold125.00 versus reclaim118.00 then115.00. Full OHLC arrays are shared by charts and tables. Intrabar order is not invented.
