## Concept brief before release

**Learner payoff:** identify the first opposing break, name its scale and distinguish the warning from its later outcome.

**Core definition:** the first qualifying break of the selected opposing swing against the tracked sequence. We use a completed close strictly beyond the swing; not every educator does.

**Sequence:** establish prior highs/lows → select opposing swing and recognition time → apply fixed wick/close test → timestamp warning → inspect separate continuation/reclaim/range branches → apply remaining setup/execution requirements or stand aside.

**Required distinctions:** bullish/bearish; protected swing as role not guaranteed barrier; internal warning versus external change; touch/wick/close/body; CHoCH/BOS/MSS/CISD; liquidity inference versus visible OHLC; event versus fill; warning versus confirmation of future direction. A higher-timeframe PD-array context can be a chosen model filter, not a universal CHoCH definition.

**Invented examples:** illustrative index, five-minute bars, America/New_York, no historical trading date. Selected108.00 low; wick107.00/close109.00; warning close106.00; branch continuation102.00, reclaim113.00 or range106.50. All branches preserve the first two bars. The107.50 lower high in the continuation branch is confirmed only after its right-hand bar closes. Numerical risk example107/110/101 is illustrative arithmetic, not a trade recommendation.

**Visual direction:** branching warning timeline plus three selectable later paths; smaller/larger reference comparison; step-specific diagrams. Contrasts are different events, not recoloured labels on a single success drawing. Text and OHLC tables remain available without script execution.
