## Pedagogical & Accuracy Safeguards
1. **Plain Definition First:** BSL = resting buy-stop orders (short stop-losses + breakout buy-stops) above highs; SSL = resting sell-stop orders (long stop-losses + breakout sell-stops) below lows.
2. **Order-Book Mechanics:** Explain clearly why a short seller's stop-loss is a *buy* order and a long buyer's stop-loss is a *sell* order — beginners often confuse this.
3. **Observable vs. Unobserved:** OHLC candles show the structural swing highs/lows and post-excursion reaction, never exact resting stop quantities or participant intent.
