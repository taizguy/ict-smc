1. **Observable vs. Inferred:** A candle chart proves that price spiked past a swing level and closed back inside (leaving a wick); it never proves deliberate manipulation or reveals participant identity.
2. **Order-Book Mechanics:** Triggered stop-losses convert into market orders; in thin book levels, cascading market orders cause slippage and fast wick spikes.
