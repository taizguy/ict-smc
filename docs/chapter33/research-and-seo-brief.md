## 2. Competitor & YouTube Coverage Gap Analysis

Most tutorials on blogs and YouTube cover only a textbook U-shape diagram with four labels (`Original Consolidation -> Sell-Side -> SMR -> Buy-Side`) and make three critical omissions that confuse beginners and even intermediate learners:

1. **No clear beginner intuition before jargon:** Beginners immediately hit terms like `IPDA`, `PD Array`, `CISD`, and `Re-Accumulation` without understanding the basic retail trap mechanics (why late shorts create exit liquidity and why symmetric consolidations act as structural waypoints).
2. **Confusion between Model 6, Model 7, and Month 7:** Learners rarely see how Charter Model 6 (Universal Buyside Expansion + 50% Discount/Equilibrium rule), Charter Model 7 (Full MMBM/MMSM Sell-Side/Buy-Side symmetry), and Core Month 7 (LRLR vs. HRLR, IPDA blending, Midweek Reversals) fit together into one coherent workflow.
3. **Lack of concrete failure branches & falsifiable checklists:** Almost no tutorial shows *when NOT to take an MMBM* (missing HTF discount context, failure to reclaim equilibrium, HRLR chop blocks, or mistaking a micro 1m bounce for an SMR) or explains that OHLC candles cannot observe true dealer inventory.
