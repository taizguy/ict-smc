## Editorial decision

Teach a time-qualified FVG entry framework, not a magical one-hour trading signal. The distinctive contribution should be a precise account of **what was known, at what time, under which written rule**. Show the same initial evidence leading to a profitable illustrative outcome, a loss, an unfilled order and a correct decision to stand aside. Do not claim exhaustive knowledge of every video or every variation.

This is not an instruction to reproduce a transcript. Write original explanations supported by concise citations. Treat institutional motives, hidden stops and a price-delivery algorithm as framework explanations, not observations established by candle geometry.

## Source conflicts to preserve openly

- Some checklists mandate a fresh sweep/MSS; the model's clock and FVG do not automatically establish every later checklist filter. Explain a declared conservative teaching variant separately.
- The original selected passage puts entry inside the hour and permits holding beyond it. Formation-in-hour but fill-later is not silently equivalent.
- First FVG, edge entry, CE entry, stop beyond sweep, gap-based stop and cancellation deadline must be versioned choices where primary evidence does not establish a universal instruction.
- “Every day somewhere” teaching claims must not become “this instrument gives a trade every day.” A fixed time window can produce no qualifying opportunity.
- Minimum offered range is not guaranteed entry-to-exit profit. Point/tick/pip units and instrument matter; recheck exact original range passage before quoting it.
- Candle patterns show traded ranges, not actual hidden stops, trader motives or a centrally directed market algorithm.
