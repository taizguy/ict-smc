## Original directions
1. Evidence plate: white/cobalt, measured hatching and annotation columns. Numerical quantities shown with countable tiles.
2. Colour-block atlas: blue/orange fields, large numbers and split compositions. Flat, no perspective or lighting.
3. Ink & paper: outlined candles, printed dots, restrained two-colour emphasis and cut-paper annotations. Paper edges are irregular; data geometry stays exact.

Each has three samples: FVG formation, touch versus fill, and MNQ calculation. Nine original SVG studies use the same invented prices for a fair comparison. 8 points / 0.25 = 32 ticks; 32 x $0.50 = $16 per MNQ contract before costs. Candle colour does not define FVG validity.
