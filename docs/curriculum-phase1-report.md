Existing `/chapter/` numeric IDs remain compatible. Internal links are normalised to canonical destinations, with source-section anchors where a known old section is linked. External old fragments without a server-visible section cannot be translated individually by HTTP; migrated links generated by the site use the new explicit anchors, while an old base URL lands at its retained topic section.

All retained lessons keep their slugs. Each language has a self-canonical and reciprocal en/ur/x-default tags. Course schema lists the 57 actual lessons, and chapter breadcrumbs follow the ten-part structure. Existing visible FAQ answers remain in schema, including retained merged answers; no rich-result eligibility is promised.
