## Collection and review scope

The supplied collection contains **41 MP4 files, approximately 24.52 hours**: an introduction and Episodes 2–41. The introduction has inventory ID 0; there is no missing Episode 1 inferred from the numbering.

The initial batch examined Episodes 3 and 6. This extension covers **the other 39 recordings at the same selected-passage review level**. It combines selected English automatic-caption passages from the official public videos with local chart-frame inspection for every recording. It does **not** claim continuous viewing of every minute, line-by-line certification of all speech, or verification of trading performance. Forty official videos supplied English automatic-caption files. Episode 28 is a silent presentation and was reviewed through six chronological frame samples instead of a fabricated transcript.

Episodes 3 and 6 have full local machine-draft transcripts from the earlier batch. Important wording in Episodes 3, 6, 18, 35 and 41 was also checked using separate local beam-size-5 transcription passes. The originals remain read-only. Public automatic captions were downloaded for local research; no local video, audio, transcript or frame was uploaded. These working files and model weights remain under ignored `outputs/`. The website publishes independent paraphrases, original exercises and links to the public sources, not the recordings or full transcripts.

## Changes and boundaries

- Specific source links and approximate passage notes accompany the relevant English and Urdu lessons; they also populate LearningResource citations in static HTML.
- Corrected the distinction between an initial swing crossing, displacement, later FVG completion and a chosen entry variant. A universal close-only or limit-only rule is not imposed across all examples.
- Expanded swing hierarchy, no-setup observations, order-block reference selection, uncertain daily bias, conditional opening-price returns and product-specific session windows.
- Corrected deterministic PO3, opening-reversal, SMT, opening-gap and final-hour descriptions. A selected chart interpretation is not proof of participant inventory or a guaranteed next move.
- Replaced unsupported compounding and universal risk prescriptions with explicitly labelled research assumptions and drawdown discussion. Retrospective journal notes are separate from genuine prior forecasts and actual execution records.
- Corrected MNQ point-versus-tick wording using CME’s official specifications; added BLS and Federal Reserve calendar references.
- Removed the remaining contradictory numbered Charter Model 13 taxonomy from the 2022 guide. Existing lesson URLs and progress identifiers are retained.
- The 2022 collection cannot authenticate every private-course or 2025/2026 label elsewhere in the curriculum. Such attribution remains explicitly unverified. The separate Abdullah Masood material is not reattributed to ICT.
- No X post-specific claims were added; the earlier access limitation remains disclosed. No account screenshots, win rates, return promises or instructor endorsements are presented as independently verified evidence.
