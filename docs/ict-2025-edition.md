# ICT 2025 edition — source and implementation record

Reviewed 26 October 2026.

## Scope and evidence

The supplied local folder contains 83 recordings (about 40 hours). The official 2025 Lecture Series playlist fetched on the review date contains the same 83 YouTube IDs. Existing caption extracts and a fresh thematic index were used to inspect selected contextual passages from all 75 captioned recordings. Eight remaining recordings were visual-only sources. This is not full continuous viewing, independent execution verification, or evidence for performance claims. Automatic-caption numbers and labels require chart checks. Original frames remain private research material and are not republished.

Six tutorial contact sheets were extracted for opening ranges, first-FVG rejection, PD-array matrix, Venom, lunch and Gauntlet. References use original titles and direct official-video URLs. NYSE, Federal Reserve, BLS, Cboe and OIC sources independently check market hours, calendars and options mechanics. Competitor pages informed explanatory structure only, not attributed ICT rules.

## Curriculum decisions

Twelve standalone lessons replace the four short Part IX notes. Venom retains its existing ID53 and canonical slug while moving into the 2025 learning order. Seven new IDs58–64 bring the whole curriculum to60 lessons. Existing IDs and URLs are preserved, including the legacy weekly-profile slug; no redirect is necessary. Sources stay below learner-facing explanations. No success rate, guaranteed gap fill, deterministic Friday reversal, institution identity from OHLC, or unsupported 81-array catalogue is asserted.

Each lesson has definitions, a worked invented-price example, contrary/no-entry outcomes, three applied quiz questions, a server-rendered comparison table, five FAQ answers shared with FAQPage schema, source references, bilingual copy and a source-labelled historical candlestick replay. Source catalogues remain the place to inspect all original demonstrations. FAQ markup makes no promise of Google rich-result eligibility.
