## Content rules

- Explanations teach the subject directly. Source timestamps and review scope belong in the references below.
- Worked values are invented, not historical OHLC or verified executions. Ordinary FVG wick geometry, body-price imbalances and session opening gaps remain distinct.
- A range multiple is not described as an estimated statistical standard deviation. A missing required close cannot be replaced with a wick touch.
- No unverified hit-rate, guaranteed target, unbreakable array or inferred institutional identity is published.
- CME specifications support the NQ/MNQ arithmetic; the BLS, Federal Reserve and NYSE pages support event/session references.
- English/Urdu content, two applied questions, a static comparison table, five FAQs, contextual links and review dates accompany each lesson.
