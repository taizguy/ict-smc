Queries were researched before the new topic routes and metadata were authored. Primary teaching facts come from the official ICT channel passages listed in each lesson, not competitor summaries.

The first three predominantly follow a lecture or transcript. LearnSMC instead uses standalone explanations, worked arithmetic and a contrasting result. The backtesting competitor makes sweeping claims about automation and minimum sample sizes; those were not adopted. Observable rules can be coded, discretion needs explicit annotation and no fixed trade count certifies an edge.

Other search observations included first-hour range articles and community indicators. These informed naming and common confusions only; no results-page claim was treated as authenticated ICT teaching.
