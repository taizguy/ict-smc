Its meta description names break of structure, liquidity sweeps, order blocks, fair value gaps and premium/discount. The page has separate headings for each, followed by sequencing, limits and FAQ. This is a concept guide, whereas LearnSMC’s target page is a navigable collection of existing focused lessons.

## Editorial decision

- Title: **ICT Masterclass: Visual Smart Money Concepts | LearnSMC** (54 characters). Preserve the complete reviewed title rather than letting the automatic width budget drop “Concepts”.
- H1: **ICT Masterclass: Visual Lessons on Smart Money Concepts**.
- Description: **Explore ICT and Smart Money Concepts lessons on PD arrays, entry models and opening ranges. Compare visual study notes, sequences and invalidation rules.** (153 characters).
- Target: a learner looking for a specific ICT/SMC model after basic candles and structure, with a beginner route for newcomers. The collection complements the curriculum rather than competing with a single FVG or order-block lesson.
- The existing URL, all lesson URLs and source data are preserved. Counts come from the live catalogue. The page is explicitly independent LearnSMC material and makes no performance or affiliation claims. No meta-keywords tag is added.
