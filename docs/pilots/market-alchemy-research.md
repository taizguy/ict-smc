### Price and orientation checks

- 28:20 / 35:45 settings frames: prior-close anchor **29,510.00** and 09:30 open **29,476.25**. Gap width **33.75** points.
- In the displayed Fibonacci orientation, **0 = 29,510.00**, **1 = 29,476.25**. Thus price at level `r` is `29,510 − r × 33.75`. Negative levels project above the prior close. Reversing orientation requires changing the level signs/formula; never present `open + 8 gaps` as the same as the source's `−8` line.
- `−6.5`: 29,729.375 unrounded; `−7`: **29,746.25** (visible at 35:45); `−7.5`: 29,763.125 unrounded; `−8`: **29,780.00** (visible at 36:40 / 39:50). Half-tick arithmetic is not itself a valid executable NQ price. Do not claim an unverified rounding policy.
- 36:40 hovered candle header shows high **29,782.00**: an overshoot of the 29,780 line, not a perfect tick-exact ceiling.
- 39:50 chart annotates a short at **29,773.25**; this is a displayed annotation, not an independently verified account fill. Exact execution timestamp not verified.
- Nearer lows visible at **29,692.25** and **29,664.50**. Their midpoint is **29,678.375** unrounded; the source's plotted 0.5 label is 29,678.50. Keep this separate from opening-gap projections.
- 21:40 frame: hover at **13:26**, candle low **29,595.25**; source-marked earlier low **29,595.50**, described at **10:29**. One tick of traversal does not establish an exit below the lower price.

## 4. Topic-specific outline and visual plan

Learner outcome: reproduce the shown opening-gap level set, explain why some touches do not qualify, distinguish the observed response from an automatic short, and record exits/failures without a hindsight target.

Sequence: ATH misconception and scope → exact two-price gap → drawing/settings and arithmetic → timing eligibility → historical overshoot/response → exit ladder and event horizon → three contrasting outcomes → separate real loss → limits and replay task. Each follows this particular source's logic; no other chapter's outline is copied.
