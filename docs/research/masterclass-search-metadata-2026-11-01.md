# Masterclass search-intent and metadata review

Reviewed: 1 November 2026. Scope: all 29 Masterclass lessons, with English and genuine Urdu metadata. Local changes only.

## Method and limits

A separate query was run for every lesson, with targeted follow-up queries where initial results were unrelated. The JSON evidence file records queries and returned result titles/URLs. Selected competitor pages were opened to inspect headings and scope. These are search-provider results, not a measured Google ranking, keyword-volume study or People Also Ask survey. No competitor trading claim was treated as factual validation of our lesson.

Google's [title-link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) calls for concise, descriptive, distinct titles without stuffing; its [snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet) calls for page-specific summaries and notes that Google may choose text from the body. Character ranges below are editorial checks, not Google eligibility thresholds.

Initial page fetches failed for CISD/Ensado, rejection block/eXpeed and Sick Sister/Edge of ICT. Other initial results were too broad or unrelated for MMXM, opening ranges, implied FVG, three-day rule, suspension block, double purge and Hangman. Follow-ups are recorded; narrow or conflicting evidence is not presented as demonstrated search demand. No new traffic, ranking, indexing or AI-citation claim is made.
