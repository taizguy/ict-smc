## Original article decisions

Five new essays use independent prose, original fictional situations, new arithmetic examples, and forty original three-step illustrations. No supplied chart screenshots, graphic assets, quotations, historical trade stories, claimed win rates or PDF files are published. Authorship is Muhammad Umer Ijaz under the user's existing authorization. ICT vocabulary is presented as charting conventions, not an endorsement or exchange-certified classification.

- **Beginner study plan:** a pencil expedition with Sam, a fictional student; product knowledge, one question, operational definitions, sequential replay and a journal.
- **Higher-timeframe bias:** a mission-control comic; conditional scenarios, relative anchors, recognition timing, independent risk rules and timestamped revisions.
- **PD arrays:** a museum sketchbook; observable geometry versus inferred participants, three-candle FVGs, candle zones, interval overlap, inversions and correlated labels.
- **Opening range versus gap:** an opening-bell theatre; date-aware New York time, product-specific references, completed versus developing ranges, and range multiples versus statistical standard deviation.
- **Entry-model comparison:** a graph-paper laboratory; complete rule sets, sequence, order assumptions, ambiguous OHLC paths, unseen evaluation and selection bias.

Public primary references are linked with a statement of what they support. CFTC material supports hypothetical-result limitations; Investor.gov supports stock-order limitations; CME material supports futures risk background; NYSE and NIST support session/timezone facts. None validates the chart models. The private PDFs remain inspiration and are not presented as public evidence for an edge.
