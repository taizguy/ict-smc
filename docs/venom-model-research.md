## Content Gaps Identified in Competing Pages

1. **Conflation of Wick Probes vs. Closing Fangs:** Many tutorials treat any brief wick above the 08:00–09:30 high as an identical Venom trigger, omitting the reviewed candle-close excursion followed by opposing inefficient departure.
2. **Unfilled Deferred Orders & Chasing Math:** Competitors rarely quantify what happens when price moves straight to the target without returning to the declared Venom open—and how chasing three points later turns a 2.33R plan into a 0.67R trade.
3. **Invented Mandatory 5-Stage Chains:** Several summaries insist every Venom trade *must* include CRT + SMT + CISD + First Presented FVG + Breaker simultaneously, whereas SMT, FVG, and Breakers are optional context layers rather than compulsory requirements for every variant.
4. **Epistemic Overclaiming:** Most external guides frame "trapped traders" and "institutional algorithms" as proven facts and advertise fixed win percentages without sample size, costs, or slippage caveats.
5. **Visual Deficiency:** Most guides present walls of text or a single static chart rather than interactive visualizers for the 08:00–09:30 clock, Two-Fang step-by-step formation, BPR overlap arithmetic, 3D multi-branch motion studies, and stick-figure mistake clinics.
